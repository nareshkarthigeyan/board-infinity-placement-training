    An Armstrong number (or narcissistic number) is a number that equals the sum of its own digits, each raised to the power of the total number of digits in the number, like 153 (\(1^{3}+5^{3}+3^{3}=153\)) or 1634 (\(1^{4}+6^{4}+3^{4}+4^{4}=1634\)).
